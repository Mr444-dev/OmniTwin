    Before enabling perception

    Save the downloaded file as local/calibration.json. Check the mapping against independent points at known positions. Validation checks geometry; accuracy requires measurement.

    Keep the same camera, resolution, and position. Moving the camera requires recalibration. Homography handles an angled view of a flat plane. If straight edges visibly bow from lens distortion, undistort the camera with a checkerboard first; do not draw curved workspace boundaries.
